PhET Interactive Simulations

A massive open-source suite of interactive math and science simulations developed by the University of Colorado Boulder under a Creative Commons license. Operates entirely in browser with zero ads, telemetry, or login walls.

Replaces: Proprietary lab software, bloated Flash applets
Tags: #open-source #educational-simulations #chemistry #physics #zero-signup

Excalidraw

Virtual whiteboard tool for hand-drawn style diagrams and quick schematics. Runs locally in the browser, supports end-to-end encryption for session sharing, and exports clean SVG/PNG files without watermarks or subscription prompts.

Replaces: Miro, Lucidchart, subscription whiteboards
Tags: #diagrams #client-side #privacy-first #e2ee #zero-signup

Open Culture

A high-yield digital archive indexing free educational and cultural media: courses, classic movies, audiobooks, eBooks, and rare historical manuscripts curated without paywalls.

Replaces: Paywalled media archives, content aggregator sites
Tags: #public-domain #education #archival #open-access
