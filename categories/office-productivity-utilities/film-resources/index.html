> FILM RESOURCES

MATCHING RECORDS: 6

  • MovieLens:
    A non-commercial, academic movie recommendation platform run by the University of Minnesota that provides ad-free, non-tracking personalized film discovery.
  • Film Colossus:
    An independent film scholarship and critique portal providing detailed breakdowns of complex film narratives, themes, and endings.
  • BFI Sight and Sound Poll:
    The official open-access digital archive of the British Film Institute's historic decade-by-decade 'Greatest Films of All Time' poll and critical essays.
  • Criterion Current:
    A free public repository of film scholarship, director essays, and historical liner notes published by The Criterion Collection.
  • WhatIsMyMovie:
    A free descriptive natural language search engine developed by University of Oulu researchers to identify forgotten films by scene descriptions.
  • JustWatch:
    A global streaming search engine that indexes title availability, rental pricing, and platform licenses across digital catalog services without requiring an account.
